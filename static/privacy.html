Bollwark · Privacy

Privacy notice

What Bollwark sees when it verifies your request, and what it doesn't.

Bollwark is self-hosted. The site you're visiting runs its own installation, so the data described here stays with that operator. It is not shared with us, with advertising networks, or with any third party.

What Bollwark receives

To decide whether to challenge a request, the widget sends — and the server records — the following:

  • IP address — used to rate-limit your requests. When the operator has loaded a reputation list, your IP may also be checked against known abusive networks (datacenter, Tor, VPN ranges).
  • Request headers — the User-Agent, Accept-Language, and Accept-Encoding your browser already sends with every request, used to detect obviously malformed clients.
  • Behavioural counters — the number of times your mouse moved, you tapped the screen, scrolled, or you pressed a key while the page was open. These are counts only — no positions, no paths, no keystrokes are recorded.
  • Automation flag — a single boolean (navigator.webdriver) your browser exposes when it's being driven by a test framework. No values beyond true/false/absent are stored.
  • Time on page — how many milliseconds elapsed between the page loading and you submitting the form, and the time to your first interaction with it.

When enabled by the operator

These signals only run when the operator has explicitly turned them on. They are off by default.

  • TLS fingerprint — when the operator runs the captcha behind a reverse proxy that emits a JA-style fingerprint (e.g. Cloudflare's cf-ja4), that fingerprint is read from the request and used to flag well-known automation tools. The fingerprint summarises the cipher-suite handshake; it does not identify you personally.
  • IP reputation list — a CIDR list maintained by the operator; your IP is compared against it, no remote lookup is performed.
  • Decision logs — when the operator enables the validation dashboard, each verification decision is written to a local SQLite file for abuse investigation. Log records include the fields above. Bollwark imposes no remote retention; the operator is responsible for pruning.

What Bollwark does not collect

  • No client-side fingerprinting libraries, canvas/WebGL probes, font enumeration, or device-ID extraction.
  • No cursor positions, click coordinates, or keystroke content.
  • No third-party tracking or advertising identifiers.
  • No cookies or other browser storage of any kind, so no cross-site profile can be built.

Retention

Individual challenges are short-lived and expire after a few minutes. Anything written to a decision log (above) is kept according to the operator's retention policy.

Cookies

Bollwark sets no cookies and uses no other browser storage. Everything it needs to verify you is computed server-side from the request itself, so there is nothing stored on your device to consent to.

Your rights

Because Bollwark is operated by the site you're visiting, any access, correction, or deletion requests should go to that site's operator. See their privacy policy for contact details.
