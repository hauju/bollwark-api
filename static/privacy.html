RustCaptcha · Privacy

Privacy notice

What RustCaptcha sees when it verifies your request, and what it doesn't.

RustCaptcha is self-hosted. The site you're visiting runs its own installation, so the data described here stays with that operator. It is not shared with us, with advertising networks, or with any third party.

What RustCaptcha receives

To decide whether to challenge a request, the widget sends — and the server records — the following:

  • IP address — used to rate-limit and to look up known abusive networks.
  • Request headers — the User-Agent, Accept-Language, and Accept-Encoding your browser already sends with every request, used to detect obviously malformed clients.
  • Trust cookie (optional, when enabled) — a single signed cookie named __captcha_trust that records the time of your last successful verification, so repeat visitors aren't re-challenged unnecessarily. It contains no personal data.
  • Behavioural counters — the number of times your mouse moved, scrolled, or you pressed a key while the page was open. These are counts only — no positions, no paths, no keystrokes are recorded.
  • Time on page — how many milliseconds elapsed between the page loading and you submitting the form.

What RustCaptcha does not collect

  • No browser fingerprinting beyond standard request headers.
  • No cursor positions, click coordinates, or keystroke content.
  • No third-party tracking or advertising identifiers.
  • No cross-site profiles. The cookie is scoped to the captcha domain and contains a single timestamp.

Retention

Individual challenges are short-lived and expire after a few minutes. Decision logs, if the operator enables them, are intended for abuse investigation and are kept according to that operator's retention policy — RustCaptcha imposes no remote retention on its own.

Cookies

The optional __captcha_trust cookie is set only when the operator enables it and only after a successful verification. It is signed (not encrypted), readable only by the captcha service, and contains a timestamp — nothing else.

Your rights

Because RustCaptcha is operated by the site you're visiting, any access, correction, or deletion requests should go to that site's operator. See their privacy policy for contact details.
